Our Story

A Reason for Being

Ikigai — a Japanese concept meaning "a reason for being." And that's exactly what we bring to every roll, every bowl, every interaction.

What started as a food truck dream has grown into St. Petersburg's favorite sushi spot. We're a small, family-owned restaurant that takes pride in fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and making every customer feel like family. From our famous Volcano Roll to our perfectly seasoned hibachi — everything is made with love and served with a smile.
